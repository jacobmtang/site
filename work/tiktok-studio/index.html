I redesigned TikTok’s creator workspace app around its Tako AI agent, with a performant native-first refresh that weighs engineering cost against time to ship. I delivered high-fidelity mockups in Figma and a real Swift app for easier handoff and real system components, built on a design library of 78 primitives, 156 semantic tokens and 22 text styles derived from the real app.
