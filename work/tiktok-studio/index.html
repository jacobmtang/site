TikTok Studio Redesign

  • Product

I redesigned TikTok’s creator workspace app around its Tako AI agent, making Studio more useful to creators and tying it more tightly into their workflow. It’s a native-first refresh that weighs engineering cost against time to ship, delivered as high-fidelity Figma mockups, a design library, and an interactive SwiftUI app built in Xcode from native system components, for easier handoff to engineering.

Current gaps现有不足

Creator Assistant feels brokenCreator Assistant 用起来像出了故障

Little context on the creator, so answers stay generic. “Sending” while a response generates reads as a network delay, some responses fail outright, and its only entry point is the Analytics overview.它对创作者了解有限，回答往往很笼统。回复生成期间显示“发送中”，让人误以为是网络延迟。部分回复会直接失败，而且唯一入口在数据分析概览页。

TikTok Tako already does moreTikTok Tako 已经做得更多

Tako in the main app already has the context of the video you’re on and suggests prompts for it, with native liquid glass and richer animations.主应用中的 Tako 已能获取你正在看的视频的上下文并推荐相关提问，还采用原生液态玻璃和更丰富的动画。

Minor overlooked details一些被忽视的小细节

Discover saves to collections that can’t be viewed, and its collections card leads nowhere. Favorites and Likes are buried in Settings. Display can’t follow the system’s light or dark mode.发现页可保存到收藏夹，但收藏夹无法查看，收藏夹卡片也点不进去。收藏和点赞藏在设置里。界面无法跟随系统浅色或深色模式。

What my redesign prioritizes本次改版的优先事项

least disruption影响最大，干扰最小

Prioritize features that add the most to creators’ workflow while minimizing disruption to existing flows users are already familiar with.优先选择最能提升创作者工作流程的功能，同时尽量减少对用户已熟悉流程的干扰。

Feasible to build,
quick to ship易于实现，快速上线

Weigh engineering cost and time to ship, and reuse what TikTok already has.权衡工程成本与上线时间，并复用 TikTok 已有的能力。

Why a native-first approach为什么采用原生优先的方式

Performant性能出色

iOS renders the glass and blur itself on the GPU, so the app adds no drawing work; scrolling triggers no layout changes, and Tako’s hub header only scales.玻璃和模糊由 iOS 自行在 GPU 上渲染，应用无需额外绘制；滚动不会引起布局变化，Tako 主页的标题只做缩放。

Easy to fall back易于降级

With no background of their own, headers become a solid bar with a hairline before iOS 26 or under Reduce Transparency, and a Material top app bar on Android.标题栏没有自己的背景，在 iOS 26 之前或开启“降低透明度”时会变成带细线的实色栏，在 Android 上则对应 Material 顶部应用栏。

Less engineering burden减轻工程负担

System bars get native support with each iOS update, from accessibility to Dynamic Type and new materials, with minimal custom code to keep in step.系统栏随每次 iOS 更新自动获得原生支持，从辅助功能到动态字体和新材质，只需维护极少的自定义代码。

Already in TikTokTikTok 已在使用

TikTok is A/B testing liquid glass bottom tabs, and TikTok Tako’s chat already uses liquid glass.TikTok 正在 A/B 测试液态玻璃底部标签栏，TikTok Tako 的聊天界面也已采用液态玻璃。

Familiar bottom tabs熟悉的底部标签

The tabs stay exactly as they ship (Home, Analytics, Create, Inbox, Manage), to minimize disruption for existing users.底部标签与现有版本完全一致（首页、数据分析、创作、收件箱、管理），尽量减少对现有用户的干扰。

Why Tako is the focus为什么以 Tako 为核心

Users are turning to personal AI agents用户正在转向个人 AI 智能体

Consumers increasingly rely on personal AI agents to discover, evaluate and buy. Building Studio around Tako keeps creators asking TikTok, not an outside AI tool.消费者越来越依赖个人 AI 智能体来发现、评估和购买。以 Tako 为核心打造 Studio，让创作者直接向 TikTok 提问，而不是转向外部 AI 工具。

Fewer new screens to build and learn更少需要开发和学习的新界面

Every new screen or major redesign adds engineering overhead and more room for bugs, and asks users to learn an unfamiliar interface. Tako can reuse what TikTok already has: TikTok Tako and CapCut already generate images in chat.每个新界面或大幅改版都会增加工程开销和出错的可能，也要求用户重新学习陌生的界面。Tako 可以复用 TikTok 现有的能力：TikTok Tako 和剪映已经支持在聊天中生成图片。

Personalized to each creator为每位创作者个性化

RAG over the creator’s content and history, which TikTok already stores, needs minimal data pipeline additions, and a lightweight living memory file resurfaces personal details when they’re relevant, reinforcing the connection.基于 TikTok 已存储的创作者内容与历史记录做 RAG 检索，只需少量新增数据管道；再配合轻量、持续更新的记忆文件，在合适的时候重提个人细节，加深彼此的联系。

Context from the first message从第一句话起就有上下文

Each session opens with an insights snapshot and algorithm suggestions already in context, so Tako’s first reply feels personal. Together with the Planner and routines, this builds a habit loop.每次对话开始时已注入数据快照和算法建议，Tako 的第一句回复就很个性化。再结合计划和例行任务，形成习惯闭环。

A familiar format for the whole workflow熟悉的形式，承载整个工作流程

Chat is familiar from every AI app, and it connects Studio’s tools. From one chat, a creator can check their numbers, get ideas, plan the week and make a cover, so Tako reinforces TikTok Studio as the all-in-one workspace.聊天是各类 AI 应用中早已熟悉的形式，也把 Studio 的各项工具串联起来。在一次对话中，创作者就能查看数据、获取灵感、安排一周计划并制作封面，让 Tako 进一步巩固 TikTok Studio 一站式工作台的定位。

New ways to monetize新的变现途径

Tako’s answers can naturally suggest Promote when a post takes off, TikTok Shop products to feature or to buy for a creator’s setup, and local businesses listed on TikTok as places to film.Tako 的回答可以自然地在作品走红时建议使用 Promote 推广，推荐可带货或用于创作者装备的 TikTok Shop 商品，以及在 TikTok 上入驻、适合拍摄的本地商家。
