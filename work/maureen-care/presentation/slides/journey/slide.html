Persona B用户画像 B

Needs dietary structure需要饮食结构

Scenario情境

Irregular meals and long gaps lead to overeating later in the day, with little reason to cook nourishing meals.三餐不规律、间隔过长，导致之后进食过量，也缺少动力去做有营养的饭菜。

Goal目标

Eat balanced meals at regular times, and make cooking at home easier.按时吃均衡的饭菜，让在家做饭变得更轻松。

Problem问题
Solution解决方案
Action行动
Outcome结果
Actions行为
Skips breakfast, grazes all afternoon, overeats at night, and reaches for takeout over cooking不吃早餐，下午零食不断，晚上吃得过量，宁愿叫外卖也不下厨
Plans the week in Meal Plan, saves dishes to the Recipe Library and adds ingredients to the Groceries list用饮食计划规划一周，把菜品存入食谱库，并把食材加入购物清单
Cooks a nourishing meal at home in step-by-step Cook Mode, paces it with the Pacer, logs it with a photo在家用分步烹饪模式做一顿有营养的饭，用节拍器放慢进食，并拍照记录
Looks back on a week of regular meals in the Log在饮食记录中回看一周的规律三餐
Thoughts想法
“I never know what to eat until I'm starving.”“不到饿极了，我从来不知道该吃什么。”
“If it's decided, I don't have to think about it.”“提前定好了，我就不用再纠结。”
“Slow down. Put the fork down.”“慢一点，先放下叉子。”
“I'm not starving by 9 pm anymore.”“晚上九点，我不再饿得发慌了。”
Feelings感受
😩Frustrated, out of control沮丧，失去掌控
😐Organised, a little skeptical有条理，但仍有些怀疑
🙂Present, steady专注当下，平稳
😊In control, at ease重获掌控，从容自在
Pain points痛点
Never sure what to eat next, and little reason to cook a nourishing meal总是不确定下一餐吃什么，也缺少动力去做一顿营养的饭
Planning takes time up front前期规划需要花时间
Eating slowly feels unnatural at first一开始慢慢吃会不太习惯
Progress is slow to show进步需要时间才能显现
